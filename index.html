Who we are

Every soul reached becomes a soul sent.

Saved To Be Sent Mission Outreach (STS) carries the Gospel into unreached villages, streets and campuses across Africa and Asia. We put God's Word in people's hands and send missionaries where Christ has not yet been named.

  • Bibles & tracts$10 puts a Bible in an unreached home.
  • MissionariesWe train, support and send workers into the field.
  • Outreach & welfareCrusades, village outreaches and care for the sick.
